🔥 Glory Beneath the Lychee Trees

In the 1950s-60s, Hainan lychee farmers carried heavy loads on shoulders, picking "branch-leaving fruit" at dawn. Every ruby-red lychee was a medal of hard work in collective cooperatives. Labor competitions and harvest offerings echoed the era's rallying cries.

📜 Red Lychee Rhymes · Cultural Heritage

From Tang-Song tribute to the "Three Wonders Lychee Stele", from revolutionary-era lychee supplies to today's "Lychee Capital" Haikou — one lychee strings together millennia of spirit and witnesses agricultural flourishing under socialist construction.

◈ Glorious Years · Red Lychee Chronicles ◈

🏅 1950s · Emancipated Lychee Growers — In early years of New China, land reform in Hainan's lychee regions gave farmers orchard shares, igniting planting enthusiasm. "Labor is glorious" echoed through the groves.
📢 1963 · Lychee Labor Model Assembly — Guangdong Province (including Hainan) held a lychee bumper harvest exchange. Labor models wore red silk flowers and presented fresh lychees to the motherland.
🍃 1980s · Tech Boosts Lychee, Happiness for All — With reform and opening, Hainan's lychee agrotech stations promoted dwarf cultivation. Lychees reached nationwide markets, allowing more workers to share sweet rewards.
✨ 2026 · Lychee Vermilion Cultural Exhibition — Hainan Provincial Museum honors the millennium-long lychee legacy, specially showcasing stories of laborers and lychees, intertwining tradition with red-spirit pride.
